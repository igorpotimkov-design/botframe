Comparison

Bot or front-desk person?

A bot does not replace a person. It takes over the repetitive work.

A bot is better when you need to

  • Answer at night or on a day off
  • Give the price and address from your list
  • Offer a free time and book it
  • Move or cancel a booking when the client asks
  • Remind about the visit the day before

A person is better when you need to

  • Handle an unusual request
  • Calm an unhappy client
  • Talk face to face and recommend a service
  • Take payment

Already have someone at the desk? The bot takes their night messages and routine questions. No one at the desk? The bot answers while you are with a client.

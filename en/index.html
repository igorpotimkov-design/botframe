Straight talk

What the bot does and does not do

Does

  • Answers questions about prices, address and services from your price list
  • Offers free times and books them
  • Tells you about every booking right away
  • Reminds the client the day before
  • Hands hard questions to you

Does not

  • Does not invent services or prices that are not in your list
  • Does not give medical advice: "I'm not a doctor. I book."
  • Does not cancel bookings or take payment by itself
  • Does not send ads. The only message it starts is a reminder about the client's own booking
Comparison

Bot or front-desk person?

A bot does not replace a person. It takes over the repetitive work.

A bot is better when you need to

  • Answer at night or on a day off
  • Give the price and address from your list
  • Offer a free time and book it
  • Remind about the visit the day before

A person is better when you need to

  • Handle an unusual request
  • Calm an unhappy client
  • Talk face to face and recommend a service
  • Take payment or cancel a booking

Already have someone at the desk? The bot takes their night messages and routine questions. No one at the desk? The bot answers while you are with a client.
